## Contributing with the general pack

- `general.work` takes any files outside `wiki/`, `notes/` and `reports/`;
  `general.report` takes Markdown under `reports/`. Wiki pages go through
  `wiki_submit`.
- Keep each candidate one reviewable unit: list only the paths that belong to it.
  Unlisted changes in your workspace stay out of the candidate.
- When the task's policy is `general.command@1`, run the configured check yourself
  before submitting; the Hub runs the same check before review and again on the
  integrated Library.
