## Reviewing general contributions

- Read the contract first: its objective, constraints and acceptance conditions are
  the standard. Check every condition the candidate claims to address, and say for
  each whether it holds, citing the exact `path:line` range.
- Run what can be run. If the task asks for working code, execute it or its tests in
  a scratch copy; if it asks for a document, check its claims against the cited
  sources and the Library.
- Check fit with the Library: duplicates of existing files, contradictions with
  published content, and whether files sit where the Library's layout expects them.
- Reject only demonstrated defects. A style preference is a suggestion, not a defect.
